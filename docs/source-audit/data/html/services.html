Something for Every Kitchen

Traditional Rice

Seeraga Samba, Kichadi Samba, Karudan Samba and other native varieties.

Siruthaniyam

Samai, Thinai, Kuthiraivali, Varagu, Kambu, Cholam and Kezhvaragu.

Paruppu & Pulses

Kadalai paruppu, thuvaram paruppu, payaru, ulundhu and kondakadalai.

Health Mixes

Kanji and dosai maavu, oottachathu mixes and ready-to-cook blends.

Natural Sweeteners

Panangkarkandu, urundai vellam and other traditional sweeteners.

Masala & Powders

Sambar, kuzhambu, rasam and milagai podi ground the traditional way.

Oils & Ghee

Cold-pressed nallennai, kadalai ennai, thengai ennai and mallamaattu nei.

Natural & Herbal

Panchakara kudineer, seekkai podi and other herbal everyday essentials.
