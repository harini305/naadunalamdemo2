## 6. Policies — the facts that must survive

**Shipping Policy** (last updated 25/08/2026)

- Packing within **24–48 h** on business days; Sunday orders pack the next business day.
- Fresh produce bought against the day's orders, limited daily quantities, slots confirmed by SMS or email.
- Delivery: **Chennai and surrounding pin codes 1–3 business days; other Tamil Nadu metros 3–5; rest of India 5–8 (non-perishable only)**. Fresh/frozen only within Chennai and selected Tamil Nadu locations.
- **Flat ₹50 per order** to every serviceable location; no handling or packaging charges.
- Failed delivery: contact to reschedule; perishables may not be re-dispatched.

**Return & Refund Policy** (25/08/2026)

- Damaged / spoiled / incorrect items: report within **24 h** of delivery with order number, product details, photos or video.
- Non-perishables: unopened, original packaging, within **7 days**.
- The same policy applies to marketplace orders from independent sellers.
- Not returnable: fresh fruit, vegetables and frozen items once delivered in acceptable condition; consumed or badly stored items.
- Natural variation in size, colour and appearance is not a defect.
